RESOURCE-TABLE SHRINK (v0.5.2):
  v0.5.2 deletes 25 resources that no localized loader referenced (early title
  bundles, early stage maps, early gallery variants, early configuration maps).
  They existed only to reproduce v0.5.1 byte for byte.  After removal those ROM
  ranges are 0xFF-filled and the ROM stays 512 KiB, because the active resources
  already reach 0x78000.  Consequently the v0.5.1 output f96fe0ec...2795 is no
  longer reproducible from this tree; the reproducible snapshot is now v0.5.2
  (95794c54...b5e6c).  Verified equivalent: 420 frames of the title screen show
  zero VRAM/BG-map/OAM/I-O difference against the pre-removal build.
